Monthly Budget Tracker (Google Sheets)

A powerful and automated budget tracking system built with Google Apps Script. This tool helps you manage your monthly finances, track daily spending, and visualize your financial health with color-coded indicators.

Key features:
✨ Automated monthly sheet generation for all 12 months (2026)    
🎨 Color-coded visual feedback for spending limits    
📊 Real-time calculations (Variable Budget, Day Limit, Days Can Survive)    
🔒 Protected ranges to prevent accidental formula deletion    
📅 Transaction tracking with date validation    
💡 Sample data generator for testing
