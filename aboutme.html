Complex ENGLISH into Simple SINHALA

An industry-level Full-Stack application developed 100% through AI collaboration and advanced Prompt Engineering—built without any prior knowledge of Next.js or Nest.js! It breaks down language barriers by extracting text from complex documents (PDF/DOCX/TXT) and generating highly structured, easy-to-understand Sinhala summaries.

Key highlights:
✨ Smart Summarization: AI-powered, categorized bullet-point summaries in Sinhala.
🎯 Interactive Magic Highlight: Clicking a summary point instantly scrolls and highlights the exact source sentence in the original document.
📄 PDF Export & Auto-Retry: 1-click native PDF downloads and intelligent progressive wait handling for API traffic limits.

Tech Stack: Next.js, Tailwind CSS, Nest.js, Google Gemini AI API, Mammoth.js.
